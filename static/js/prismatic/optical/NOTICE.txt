Majboroda / Poly Haven, CC0. https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_09

VINLUME laboratory derivatives; reference photographs are not shipped.

liquid-shader.js adapts the F06 liquid.js equations without a new dependency.
The glass controls use native backdrop blur. Native HTML retains labels,
focus, input handling and commercial actions.
